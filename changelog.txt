# Create: Railward

## Changelog:

### 0.7.1
    - Added Ponder scenes for signals, the Cistern Hatch, Fluid Terminal, Bulk Cargo Containers, Unloading Hatch and Unloading Bunker.
    - Fixed a startup crash with Create 6.0.8 on Minecraft 1.20.1.

### 0.7.0
    - Added Minecraft 1.19.2 and 1.20.1 support, including Create 6.
    - Fixed rendering and client-server synchronization issues in the newer ports.

### 0.6.4
    - Added multiple-arrival selection to the CC:Tweaked station peripheral.

### 0.6.3
    - Added a music disc.
    - Fixed dual-panel and smoke rendering; made exhaust render distance configurable.

### 0.6.2
    - Corrected Forge 40.3.12 requirements.
    - Fixed second-panel placement and added block-shape outlines.

### 0.6.1
    - Fixed particle compatibility with Forge 40.2.21.
    - Updated Forge to 40.3.12.

### 0.6.0
    - Added the radiator fan, cab corner doors, dual panels, diesel exhaust, windshield, ladder panels and train stairs.
    - Added a buffer-light variant and improved roof placement.
    - Fixed door opening sides at stations, particle rendering and textures.

### 0.5.1
    - Added gray, red and yellow train floors.
    - Fixed pilot rendering and textures.

### 0.5.0
    - Added the Modern Train Controller and fixed its behavior and rendering.

### 0.4.2
    - Added a Modern Whistle recipe using Create's whistle.
    - Fixed whistle behavior, spotlight rendering and surface-mounted block mirroring.

### 0.4.1
    - Fixed rotation and placement of surface-mounted blocks on contraptions.
    - Fixed pickaxe mining for the Cab Signal.

### 0.4.0
    - Added railings, hood walls, copycat parts, pilots, two bogeys, buffer lights and a dynamically lit spotlight.
    - Added the Cab Signal.
    - Updated roof shapes and textures.

### 0.3.12
    - Updated the version number from 0.3.8.

### 0.3.8
    - Increased whistle range to 128 blocks.
    - Added CC:Tweaked station arrivals and schedule data.
    - Improved arrival/departure predictions and compatibility with world-time changes.

### 0.3.6
    - Fixed whistle signs triggering Create's whistle.

### 0.3.5

    - Fixed cargo and fluid preservation during disassembly and improved synchronization.
    - Fixed rotation and mirroring of containers, cisterns and whistles.
    - Improved Modern Whistle integration with train arrival sounds.

### 0.3.4
    - Fixed speed-sign and whistle-sign recipes.
    - Fixed dedicated-server issues with signs and buffer lights.

### 0.3.3
    - Fixed locomotive buffer lights.

### 0.3.2

    - Fixed train-door issues.

### 0.3.0

### 0.2.1

### 0.1.4

### 0.1.3
